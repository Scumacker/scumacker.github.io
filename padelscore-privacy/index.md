# Privacy policy for PadelScore

Last updated: 16.11.2026 (version 4.0.0)

PadelScore is a padel scoring app for Android, Wear OS, iPhone and Apple Watch. This document
describes what the apps store, what leaves the device, and why. [Deutsche Fassung](datenschutz)

## What the app stores

- Your matches (team names you type in, the order of points with their time, the rules) and your
  settings are stored only on your phone and your watch, inside the app's private storage.
- There is no account, no login and no PadelScore server. The developer never receives your matches.
- Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Watch and phone

The live match is exchanged between your own phone and your own watch: on Android through the Wearable
Data Layer of Google Play services, on iPhone through Apple's WatchConnectivity. The data stays between
your paired devices.

## Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): when the app is
opened and before the summary at the end of a match, never during a match.

- To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes device data such as the IP address,
  the advertising identifier (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA), device and app information, and
  ad interactions. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and
  https://policies.google.com/privacy.
- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown
  (Google's consent form, IAB TCF). You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.
- On iPhone, the system asks whether the app may use the advertising identifier for tracking. If you
  decline, ads are not personalised with it.
- Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use the data needed to
  deliver an ad and to prevent fraud.

## No other data processing

No analytics, no crash reporting, no location, no contacts, no health data. The app does not sell data.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: write to the contact address given in the store listing.
